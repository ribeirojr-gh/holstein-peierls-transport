## PBC gauge nuance

The correct IP1n statement is therefore **zero post-switch electric field and zero external work with a continuously held boundary twist**, rather than globally gauge-identical zero-phase PBC dynamics.

This does not affect the energy-closure result, but it further motivates a control in which electronic redistribution is removed explicitly.
