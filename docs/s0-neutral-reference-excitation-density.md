# S0 neutral-reference excitation-density convention

## Why a reference subtraction is required

The historical molecular-crystal Holstein-Peierls Hamiltonian describes lattice
coordinates measured from the neutral molecular equilibrium and then adds the
electronic coupling associated with an excess carrier.  A many-electron
open-shell exciton calculation is different: the neutral occupied electronic
background is present explicitly.

If the full many-electron density were inserted directly into the old Holstein
and Peierls force expressions, the neutral background would spuriously drive the
lattice.  The S0 excited-state surface therefore uses a neutral-reference
subtraction at the **same instantaneous geometry**:

`Delta E_el(q) = E_excited(q) - E_neutral(q)`

and

`E_ref(q) = E_lattice(q) + Delta E_el(q)`.

For one HOMO-to-LUMO neutral excitation, the neutral reference contains
`n_closed+1` doubly occupied orbitals.  The open-shell singlet contains
`n_closed` doubly occupied orbitals plus two singly occupied frontier orbitals;
the high-spin triplet has the same electron number with the two singly occupied
frontier orbitals in one high-spin shell.

The corresponding spin-summed one-body density matrices satisfy

`Delta gamma = gamma_excited - gamma_neutral`

and

`Tr(Delta gamma) = 0`.

Thus the lattice couples to the redistribution generated by the neutral
excitation, not to all occupied electrons.

This choice is also aligned with the physical statement emphasized in modern
exciton-polaron theory: an exciton is charge neutral overall, but spatial
fluctuations/redistribution of its charge density can couple to and distort the
lattice.  See Z. Dai, C. Lian, J. Lafuente-Bartolome, and F. Giustino, Phys. Rev.
Lett. 132, 036902 (2024), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.132.036902.  In molecular
crystals, derivatives of excitonic Hamiltonian matrix elements with nuclear
coordinates are likewise identified with Holstein and Peierls exciton-phonon
couplings; see A. F. Morrison and J. M. Herbert, J. Chem. Phys. 146, 224110
(2017), DOI 10.1063/1.4985607.

The present implementation remains an effective-model control, not a
first-principles exciton-phonon parameterization.

## Referenced structural derivative

The density-density interaction kernel is frozen with respect to the classical
coordinates during S0, consistently with the existing static correlated sector.
Because the neutral and excited orbitals are separately variationally optimized,
the Hellmann-Feynman derivative of the referenced electronic energy depends only
on `Delta gamma`.

For the local coordinate,

`dE_ref/du_i = K1 u_i + alpha_intra Delta gamma_ii`.

For the x Peierls coordinate,

`dE_ref/dvx_i = K2(2 vx_i - vx_left - vx_right)`

`                + 2 alpha_x (Delta gamma_i,left - Delta gamma_i,right)`,

with the analogous y expression.

These are exactly the old excess-carrier force forms with the excess one-body
density replaced by the neutral excitation density matrix.

## Canonical isotropic S0 control

The extensive new implementation remains in the deliberately reduced parameter
space requested for S0:

- `J1 = J2 = 0.100 eV`;
- `alpha1 = alpha2 = 3.0 eV/A` under the S0 mapping documented previously;
- `K1 = 16.51 eV/A^2` and `K2 = 0.51 eV/A^2` remain distinct.

For exchange/correlation validation only, a simple isotropic density-density
kernel is available with independent onsite `U` and cardinal nearest-neighbour
`V`.  These values are numerical controls and are not material fits.

## Validation gates

`spin_adapted.excitation_reference` implements the neutral reference and the
following gates:

1. neutral and excited states have identical electron number;
2. `Tr(Delta gamma)=0` for both singlet and triplet;
3. when the electron-electron interaction is disabled, corresponding singlet and
   triplet excitation energies are degenerate;
4. the analytic `u`, `vx`, and `vy` derivatives of the referenced surface agree
   with central finite differences in which both electronic states are
   reoptimized at each displaced geometry;
5. the existing elastic energy is retained without adding a second neutral
   background force.

After these gates pass, the remaining S0 task is the first simultaneous
singlet/triplet + lattice relaxation and its finite-size/branch validation.
Only after that should S0 be promoted and O0 occupation/yield work begin.
